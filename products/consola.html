What is CONSOLA?

CONSOLA is a multiband console saturation plug-in for macOS, in AU and VST3. Four bands of console saturation with 16 voices that remember what came before, room to host your own plug-ins inside each band, and the JUJU limiter with PULSE mode. Built for mixing and mastering, with 5 ms of latency.
