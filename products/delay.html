What is SIMLAF Delay?

SIMLAF Delay is a delay plug-in for macOS, in AU and VST3, plus a standalone app. Stereo, ping-pong, tape, dub, multi-tap and reverse delays, locked to tempo with a groove control, plus ducking and freeze.

What it does

01

6 modes

Stereo, Ping-Pong, Tape, Dub, Multi-Tap and Reverse.

02

Tempo sync and groove

Lock repeats to the session tempo, with a groove control.

03

Ducking and freeze

Keep echoes out of the way of the dry part, or hold them.

04

Zero latency

The dry signal is never delayed.
