What is SIMLAF Tremolo?

SIMLAF Tremolo is a tremolo and auto-pan plug-in for macOS, in AU and VST3, plus a standalone app. Three kinds of rhythmic movement: level, position, and the split-band harmonic tremolo.

What it does

01

Tremolo

Rhythmic level movement.

02

Auto-pan

Move the sound across the stereo field.

03

Harmonic tremolo

Low and high bands moving against each other.
