What is TUFANI?

TUFANI is a guitar amp plug-in for macOS, in AU and VST3, plus a standalone app. 52 amps whose tone stacks are computed from their component values, with speakers, movable mics, your own impulse responses, 77 effects and a tuner. Plays as a plug-in or as a standalone app.
