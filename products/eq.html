What is SIMLAF EQ?

SIMLAF EQ is a 20-band dynamic EQ plug-in for macOS, in AU and VST3, plus a standalone app. Twenty bands, nine shapes and slopes from 6 to 96 dB/oct, on bells and shelves too. Any band can be dynamic, and any band can work on stereo, left, right, mid or side.
