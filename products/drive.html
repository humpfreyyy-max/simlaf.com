What is SIMLAF Drive?

SIMLAF Drive is a saturation plug-in, in AU and VST3, plus a standalone app. Nine saturation models with oversampling up to 8×, minimum phase by default so it adds no latency; linear phase is there if you want it. Auto gain matches loudness, so you judge the colour and not the volume.
