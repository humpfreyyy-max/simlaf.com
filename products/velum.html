What is VELUM?

VELUM is an effects rack for keys and synths, in AU and VST3, plus a standalone app. A rotor that speeds up and slows down like the real thing, a 10-band pedal EQ, oversampled colour, tape wear, echoes and rooms, and UnEcho.
