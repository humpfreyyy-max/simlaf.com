What is the SIMLAF FX Suite?

Every stock effect in the SIMLAF FX range, from the 20-band dynamic EQ and the true-peak Limiter to Tune, installed together. All of them run at zero latency by default except Tune, which needs 6–15 ms in low-latency mode and tells your host so. SIMLAF Spectral is a flagship and is sold on its own.

The five big effects (EQ, Compressor, Multiband, Limiter, Reverb) are $49 each on their own; the other 18 are $29 each.
