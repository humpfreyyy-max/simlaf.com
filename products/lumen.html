What is LUMEN?

LUMEN is an additive resynthesis synth for macOS, in AU, VST3 and CLAP, plus a standalone app. An additive synth that can rebuild any recording from its partials, with stereo phase, or turn an image into sound. Spectral filters and 372 designed presets.
