What is SIMLAF Transient?

SIMLAF Transient is a transient shaper plug-in for macOS, in AU and VST3, plus a standalone app. Bring the hit forward or pull it back, lengthen or shorten the ring, and focus the change on part of the spectrum.

By the numbers

0samplesLatency by default

What it does

01

Attack and sustain

Shape the front and the tail separately.

02

Band focus

Aim the shaping at the part of the spectrum that needs it.

03

Zero latency by default

Nothing for your host to compensate.
