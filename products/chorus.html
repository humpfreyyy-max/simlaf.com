What is SIMLAF Chorus?

SIMLAF Chorus is a chorus plug-in for macOS, in AU and VST3, plus a standalone app. Chorus, Ensemble, Dimension, Vibrato and Tri-Chorus, with a view of every voice's delay and of the stereo field.
