What is NGOMA 2?

NGOMA 2 is a drum synthesiser and beat workstation for macOS, coming January 2027. 8,580 sounds in 17 style folders, 20 kits, a log-drum engine and 16 outputs, with a workstation for building beats. NGOMA is a drum synthesiser: drop in your own one-shot and it fits its physical models to the sound.

An Amapiano groove at 112 BPM, played entirely by NGOMA: log drum, kick, shakers, claps and congas (kit: Midnight Amapiano).

What it does

01

8,580 sounds

Learns from your one-shot

Drop in a sample and NGOMA analyses it and fits its physical models to it, so the sound stays playable rather than fixed.

Log-drum engine
