Actual NGOMA 2 interface

What is NGOMA 2?

NGOMA 2 is a drum synthesiser and beat workstation for macOS, in AU and VST3, plus a standalone app. Five physical engines — modal, mesh, modal with mesh, log drum and particle — across fifteen instrument families, with 8,580 factory instruments filed by production genre, 20 kits of 32 pads and 16 direct stereo outputs beside the main pair. Drop in your own one-shot and NGOMA fits a physical model to it.

Our own clip · NGOMA 2

The log drum, modelled.

An Amapiano groove at 112 BPM, played entirely by NGOMA: log drum, kick, shakers, claps and congas (kit: Midnight Amapiano).

What it does

01

Five engines, fifteen families

Modal, mesh, modal with mesh, log drum and particle, driving kicks, snares, rims, claps, hats, cymbals, toms, congas, hand drums, log drums, bells, shakers, tines, mallets and objects. Every pad is a set of physical quantities, not a sample.

02

It fits a model to your own one-shot

Drop in a sample and NGOMA scores all 8,580 factory models against it on a 20-band spectrum and a 24-point envelope, then hill-climbs eight physical parameters over two rounds — and prints the distance it reached, so you can see how close it got.

03

8,580 instruments, filed by genre

300 authored and measured sounds in the Legacy folder, and bounded physical variants of them in seventeen production folders: amapiano, afrobeats, afro-house, afro-tech, 3-step, gqom, benga, dancehall, reggaeton, trap, drill, hip-hop, R&B, UK garage, Jersey club, drum & bass and house.

04

20 kits, 32 pads, 16 direct outs

A pattern and arrangement workstation over the kit, and sixteen direct stereo outputs beside the main pair so every piece can be mixed in your DAW.
