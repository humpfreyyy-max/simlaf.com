What is TECTON 2?

TECTON 2 is a bass amp plug-in for macOS, in AU and VST3, plus a standalone app. A complete bass rig, from synth-bass and 808 amps to cabinets, mics and effects, with tools that keep the low end readable on small speakers. Plays as a plug-in or as a standalone app for practice and recording.

What it does

01

120 amps, two at once

40 families including synth-bass and 808, with two amps in parallel.

02

36 cabs, 12 mics

Build the cabinet and the mic setup behind each amp.

03

150 effects in 8 slots

And 221 ready-made sounds.

04

Translation, Foundation, Sub Direct

Translation adds harmonics so bass reads on small speakers; Sub Direct runs the low band around the cab.

05

Standalone app

With looper, metronome and DI recording.
