How it works

  1. Each test has two versions of the same audio. The page shuffles them into A and B every time you start.
  2. X is A or B, chosen at random. Switch between A, B and X as often as you like; playback keeps its place.
  3. Say whether X is A or B, then say which version is which.
  4. Reveal. You see the names and how you did. Shuffle and go again.

The rules we follow

  • Both versions are matched for loudness before they are published.
  • Names stay hidden until you answer.
  • Nothing is played automatically.
  • Your answers stay in your browser. Nothing is sent anywhere.
