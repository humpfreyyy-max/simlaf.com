Installing

How do I install a SIMLAF product?

Download the macOS installer from the product page and open it. It puts each format where your host looks for it:

  • Audio Unit: /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components
  • VST3: /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3
  • Standalone apps, where a product has one: /Applications

Then open your host. Most hosts find new plug-ins on the next launch; some need a rescan from their plug-in settings.

My host does not show the plug-in

Quit and reopen the host, then run its plug-in rescan. Hosts that use Audio Units check each new plug-in before listing it; if a check failed, reset and rescan that plug-in from the host’s plug-in manager. Make sure you are looking for a format your host supports: some hosts load only Audio Units, others only VST3. If it still does not appear, write to us with your macOS version and host.

What are the system requirements?
  • macOS 10.13 or later; built for Intel and Apple Silicon (Apple Silicon testing in progress).
  • A host that loads Audio Unit or VST3 plug-ins. There is no AAX version. LUMEN also comes as CLAP. Products with a standalone app run without a host.
  • An internet connection once per computer, to activate.

macOS now · Windows coming December 2026 · Linux and mobile apps to follow. Each product page lists the formats that product comes in.

Is SIMLAF coming to Windows, Linux and mobile?

Yes. Windows builds arrive in December 2026, with Linux and mobile apps to follow. Today every product runs on macOS 10.13 or later; built for Intel and Apple Silicon (Apple Silicon testing in progress).

Licences and activation

How does activation work?

After you buy, you receive your licence details by email. The first time you open the product on a computer, enter them to activate. Activation needs the internet once; after that the product works offline, so you can take it to a studio with no connection.

How many computers can I use?

Your licence is for you, on up to 3 of your own computers at the same time: for example a studio Mac, a laptop and a spare. To move to a new computer when all 3 are in use, deactivate one you no longer use, or write to us and we will help.

Can someone else use my licence?

No. Licences are per user, so each person who uses SIMLAF software needs their own. The full terms are in the licence agreement.

Where do I find my orders and licence keys?

Use Sign in, at the top of any page. It takes you to our reseller’s customer portal, where you can see your orders and your licence keys. This website has no accounts of its own and never asks for a password.

Someone messaged me claiming to be SIMLAF

We never ask for your password, payment details or licence key, by email, by direct message or anywhere else, and our moderators in The SIMLAF Room never message you first about your account. If someone does, do not reply and do not click their links. Forward the message to hello@simlaf.com so we can warn others, and report the account on the platform where it reached you.

Download SIMLAF software only from this website, the link in your order email, or Sign in. A “free” or “unlocked” copy offered anywhere else is not ours and may not be safe.

Latency

What does “zero latency by default” mean?

The plug-in reports 0 samples of latency to your host with its default settings, so nothing is delayed and nothing needs compensating. Some options need time to work, such as linear phase or look-ahead. Those are off until you switch them on, and when you do, the plug-in reports exactly how much latency it adds so your host can line everything up.

Which products have latency?
  • SIMLAF Tune: 6–15 ms in low-latency mode
  • hCHOP STICKS: 104 samples at 48 kHz
  • TECTON 2: 6 samples
  • TUFANI: 6 samples
  • CONSOLA: 5 ms
  • hVOLT: 1,096 samples
  • KARAKANA: 68 samples at 48 kHz · 112 with Tape
  • UKWELI 2: 72 samples at 48 kHz · Tight Bass adds 40 ms

The other 22 SIMLAF FX effects, and SIMLAF Spectral and KAMILI, run at 0 samples by default. Each product page shows its latency where one is published.
