House rules

Short, and enforced

  1. Be kind. Feedback is about the music, never the person.
  2. Give before you ask: notes on two mixes for every one you post.
  3. No piracy. No cracked software, no requests for it, no links to it.
  4. No spam, and no self-promotion outside #show-your-work.
  5. Keep help in the help channels, so answers can be found later.
  6. Never post a licence key, an order number or an email address in public.
  7. You must be 16 or older to join.

Your safety

We will never ask for your password

  • Not your password, not your payment details, not your licence key: not in a DM, not in a channel, not anywhere.
  • Moderators never message you first about your account or a licence.
  • Your orders and licence keys live behind Sign in, on our store's customer portal. Licence questions go to hello@simlaf.com.
  • Download SIMLAF software only from simlaf.com, the link in your order email, or Sign in. A “free” copy offered anywhere else is not ours.
  • If someone claiming to be us asks for any of this, it is not us. Report them to a moderator and to Discord.
