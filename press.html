Press kit.

SIMLAF is a software company. Its audio division, SIMLAF Audio, makes audio products for people who make music: instruments, effects and the SIMLAF 2 DAW, with the numbers behind each product published. 22 of its 23 SIMLAF FX stock effects run at zero latency by default. Engineered by H. Domboka. simlaf.com

Press contact: hello@simlaf.com

Key facts

  • 34 audio products at launch on 27 November 2026: 11 flagships at $79 and 23 SIMLAF FX stock effects; KAMILI and KARAKANA follow when each is released.
  • The Whole Studio: all 34 at launch, KAMILI and KARAKANA when each is released, plus SIMLAF 2 when it ships in December 2026. $199 until 31 January 2027, then $399.
  • Zero latency by default on 22 of the 23 SIMLAF FX stock effects.
  • macOS 10.13 or later. AU and VST3; most also run standalone, and LUMEN adds CLAP. Windows planned for December 2026.
  • No subscriptions. A 14-day free trial of every product, and free updates within each major version.

About SIMLAF, in 100 words

SIMLAF is a software company. Its audio division, SIMLAF Audio, makes instruments, effects and a DAW for people who make music. The launch catalogue has 34 audio products for macOS: 11 flagships, among them bass and guitar amp suites, a vocal chain, an additive synth and modelled electric pianos, and 23 SIMLAF FX stock effects, 22 of them at zero latency by default. KAMILI and KARAKANA follow when each is released. SIMLAF 2, a Mac DAW with always-on audio and MIDI capture, is planned for December 2026; Windows versions are planned for December 2026. Engineered by H. Domboka. simlaf.com
