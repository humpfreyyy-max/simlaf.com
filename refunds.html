Refunds

Try free for 14 days first. If a product does not work on your system, ask for a refund within 14 days of purchase.

Last updated 17 October 2026.

Try first, for 14 days

Every SIMLAF product has a free 14-day trial. Please use it to check that the product works on your computer and in your host before you buy. A trial never turns into a paid purchase by itself.

If it does not work on your system

If a product you bought does not work on your system, you can ask for a refund within 14 days of purchase.

  1. Write to hello@simlaf.com with your order reference, your Mac model, your macOS version, your host and what happens when you use the product.
  2. We will try to get it working with you first. Many problems turn out to be an installation or host setting that can be fixed quickly.
  3. If we cannot make it work on your system, we refund the full price. The refund is issued by our reseller, the merchant of record, to the payment method you used, including any tax you were charged.

When a refund is issued, the licence is deactivated and the product must be uninstalled.

Bundles

The Whole Studio, the Producer Pack and the SIMLAF FX Suite are refunded as a whole: the refund covers the full bundle price and deactivates every licence in the bundle.

Duplicate purchases

If you bought the same product twice by mistake, write to us and we will refund the duplicate.

Your rights

This policy does not affect any rights you have under the consumer law of the country where you live.

Contact

Refund requests and questions: hello@simlaf.com.
