Privacy

What we collect, why, and your rights. No cookies, no analytics, no advertising trackers on this website.

Last updated 17 October 2026. This notice explains what personal data SIMLAF collects, why, and what you can do about it. We collect as little as we can.

1. Who is responsible

SIMLAF is responsible for the personal data described here. Contact us about privacy at hello@simlaf.com.

2. What we collect and why

Email sign-ups

When you ask for SIMLAF Spectral Free, sign up for the weekly SIMLAF Academy lesson, or ask to be told when a product is released, we collect your email address, the list you signed up to and the time you signed up. We use it only to send what you asked for. Every email we send has an unsubscribe link, and unsubscribing deletes you from that list.

Legal basis: your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by our reseller, the merchant of record, which collects your payment details and billing information under its own privacy notice, shown at checkout. The reseller passes us what we need to issue and support your licence: your name, email address, country, the products you bought and the order reference. We never receive your full card details.

Legal basis: performing our contract with you, and our legal obligations.

Signing in

Sign in, at the top of every page, takes you to our reseller’s customer portal, where you can see your orders and licence keys. Signing in happens there, under the reseller’s own privacy notice. This website has no user accounts, sets no login cookies and never receives your password.

Licence activation

Activating a licence connects to our licence service once per computer. It sends your licence details and an identifier for that computer, so that activations can be counted against the 3-computer limit. After activation the software does not need to connect again.

Legal basis: performing our contract with you.

Support

If you write to us, we keep your message and our reply so we can help you, and so we have the history if you write again.

Legal basis: our legitimate interest in answering you, and performing our contract where it concerns a purchase.

Our community on Discord

The SIMLAF Room is a server on Discord, for people aged 16 and over. If you join, Discord processes your account and your messages under its own terms and privacy policy. Within the server we see your Discord name and what you post there, and moderators may remove posts or members that break the server’s rules. If a moderator acts on a rule break, we keep a short record (the Discord user ID, links to the messages and screenshots) so we can handle repeat problems and reports. If you ask for the Customer role, you email us from the address you bought with and we check it against our order records. That email is kept as a support message (see Support, above); in Discord we keep only the role. We never ask for your licence key, password or payment details there. This website does not embed Discord and sends it nothing; the join link simply takes you to Discord.

Legal basis: our legitimate interest in running a safe, useful community that you chose to join.

This website

This website uses no cookies, no analytics and no advertising trackers. It does load its fonts from Google Fonts, so when you visit, Google receives your IP address and basic browser information, as described in Google’s own privacy policy. Our hosting provider keeps short-lived server logs (such as IP address, page requested and time) to deliver the site and protect it from abuse.

Legal basis: our legitimate interest in running a working, secure website.

3. Who we share it with

We share personal data only with the service providers who help us do the things above, and only what each one needs:

  • our reseller, the merchant of record, for orders, payments, tax and refunds;
  • our email service, to send sign-up emails;
  • our licence service, to activate licences;
  • our website host, to serve this website.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not share it for advertising.

4. International transfers

Our service providers may process data in countries other than yours. Where that happens, we rely on the safeguards the law requires, such as standard contractual clauses.

5. How long we keep it

  • Sign-up emails: until you unsubscribe.
  • Purchase and licence records: for as long as your licence is in use, and afterwards for as long as tax and accounting law requires.
  • Support messages: for as long as they are useful for helping you, after which we delete them.
  • Community moderation records: up to 12 months after the last incident they concern.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you can ask us to give you a copy of your personal data, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to how we use it, or send it to another service. You can withdraw consent at any time. Write to hello@simlaf.com and we will respond within the time the law allows. You also have the right to complain to the data protection authority where you live.

7. Children

Our website and products are not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their personal data.

8. Security

We use providers that protect data in transit and at rest, keep access to personal data to the people who need it, and collect as little as possible in the first place.

9. Changes

If we change how we use personal data, we will update this notice and the date at the top. If a change is significant, we will tell the people it affects.
